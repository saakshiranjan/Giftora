Our Story

Crafted from Passion, Delivered with Love

Giftora was born from a simple belief — that gifts should feel as special as the people receiving them. What started as a hobby in our living room has blossomed into a brand trusted by thousands across India.

Each product is made by hand, using carefully sourced materials, and wrapped with the same care you'd give your dearest friend. From pipe-cleaner bouquets that never wilt, to crochet flowers that spark joy — we create gifts that last forever, just like your memories.
